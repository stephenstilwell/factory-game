# Personal protection and regional conversations

Engineer [P] → Equipment includes the **Warden protective coat**. Tier one costs 20 iron plates and 10 wire after Supply routes and workshop restoration; it reduces incoming personal damage by 15%. Tier two costs 20 steel and 10 gears after Foundry craft and reduces damage by 30%. These are permanent manufactured upgrades, with the existing material tracker, new/ready indicators and save validation.

Protection applies to the engineer only. Damage rounds to the nearest integer and damaging hits always inflict at least one health. Existing invulnerability, regeneration, retreat and wave rules remain in effect. Zero-damage calls do not interrupt regeneration. Weapon handling remains a separate balance decision.

Three optional keeper conversations connect the regional loops to the town story:

- Oren introduces maintainable local electricity after Foundry craft.
- Mira responds to the first completed Orchard produce sale.
- Oren marks the ninth and final Greybank road shipment.

They appear through the existing unread journal and relevant keeper, remain replayable after reading and grant no free supplies or currency. Their prerequisites derive from persistent town progress; no new story timers or interruptions are added.
