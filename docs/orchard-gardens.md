# Lost Orchard gardens

Recover the Lost Orchard, finish the industrial opening and restore the trading post. With town services online, visit the orchard entrance and open Discoveries [L].

Restore up to three growing beds. Each costs 10 stone, 8 planks and 15 credits and grants 250 Gathering XP once. Each active bed consumes one plank and one coal/charcoal fuel to grow four fresh produce in 45 seconds. There is no hunger system or offline income.

The entrance holds up to ten planks and ten fuel. Harvest storage holds 24 produce. Growth reserves storage before consuming supplies; full storage or insufficient inputs pauses new crops. A bed added during growth joins the next crop, preserving the paid yield. Loss of town services pauses growth and market sales.

Supply, collect harvest or withdraw unused inputs through the orchard panel. For automation, place an input inserter west of the entrance facing east, and an output inserter north of the entrance facing north. The same entrance is both the receiving hatch and harvest source.

At the restored market, deliver produce through its town panel or an inserter. The market buffers up to 16 produce. Four produce fund a separate 30-second shipment paying six credits. Incomplete batches remain buffered. These sales run alongside the existing machine-parts contract.

The orchard is an ongoing agricultural supply chain; Greybank remains a finite road restoration project. Neither generates supplies for free. Beds, physical inventories, paid crops, market shipments and currency ledgers persist through saving. Older saves begin with no restored beds. Older industrial worlds can now collect four timber from each finite tree and hand-craft planks without restarting progression.
