# Settlement ages

Every world begins in Timber & Hearth with no items or machines. Supply the first shelter to establish Stone & Water and unlock managed forestry, the waterwheel sawmill and charcoal clamp. Rebuild the crossing with the Greybank mason pattern to enter Iron & Industry.

Town [Y] presents these same projects from its overview and civic panels. There is no second age project at Town Hall. Stone civic visuals and the two-credit market shipment premium remain; income still requires supplied shipments.

The early chapter and civic age must agree in saves. All three civic sites and the full opening state are required. Older formats are unsupported; see [development saves](development-saves.md).

Future chapters should extend this progression rather than maintain a parallel age system. Tests cover the empty opening, material costs, logistics milestones, crossing prerequisites, market premiums, save consistency and Town UI.
