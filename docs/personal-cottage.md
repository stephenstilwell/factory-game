# Personal cottage

Creative building starts with an optional private cottage. It is separate from civic repair requirements and factory output: no comfort score, production bonus, hunger, sleeping requirement or recurring upkeep.

## Building and entering

After completing the first shelter, open **Town [Y]** and make a cottage kit for **12 timber, 12 planks and 8 stone**. Existing industrial worlds unlock the kit when the trading post is restored. Assign the kit from the pack or Buildings picker to a hotbar slot, then place it like another building. An assembler can also manufacture the same kit later.

There is one personal cottage entrance per world. Inspect it nearby and choose **Enter cottage / decorate**. The entrance is a one-tile world building; its private ten-by-eight room is an abstract interior instance, not an outdoor footprint. Outdoor simulation, defenses, regeneration and autosave time pause while the room is open. Leaving restores the previous pause state. A disabled entrance needs repair.

Picking up and relocating the entrance returns its physical building item and preserves the entire interior and home collection. Furniture is not lost if the entrance is damaged. The cottage does not alter town buffs or enemy objectives.

## Furnishing

Choose from twelve pieces: chair, dining table, bed, bookcase, copper lantern, leafy planter, bench, fireplace, divider, keepsake box, display stand and woven rug. Each has a purpose description and exact material cost. **Make** consumes carried resources and adds one furnishing to the home collection. Rugs barter four fresh produce through an operating restored market. Decorative containers are not factory storage; lanterns and fireplaces need no electricity or fuel.

- Left-click the room to place an owned piece.
- **R** rotates its footprint and artwork.
- Right-click a piece to pick it up and select that furnishing for placement elsewhere.
- **Q** clears the placement cursor.
- **Ctrl+Z** undoes; **Ctrl+Y / Ctrl+Shift+Z** redoes room edits.
- Use **Save game** inside the room for explicit save feedback. **Esc** leaves.

Placement previews distinguish valid and blocked tiles. Furniture respects its complete rectangular footprint; rugs occupy a separate floor layer and may sit beneath furniture. The two-tile doorway stays clear. Pickup prefers furniture above a rug. No furniture can consume or duplicate factory buildings.

History holds 32 edits for placement, pickup and finishes. Making a new furnishing clears history, so undo cannot reverse a material payment or duplicate ownership. The home collection is bounded to 256 stored and placed furnishings. There is no furniture-selling or disposal loop in this slice.

## Finishes and persistence

Timber and cream are available immediately. Recovering the Lost Orchard unlocks greens; Greybank Quarry unlocks stone finishes. Floor and wall palettes can be mixed freely once discovered. Changing finishes is free and undoable.

Validated saves include stock, piece positions/rotations and finishes. Loading begins outside with fresh history. The home section is required even for an empty collection; no cottage or furniture is granted.
