# Ironworks v0.9.0 — District Power & Regional Restoration

Download the Windows x86-64 ZIP, extract the whole folder, and run Ironworks.exe. Godot is not required. The SHA256SUMS.txt asset verifies the ZIP.

## New in this build

- District electricity: material-funded charcoal dynamos, power poles and optional double-speed electric drives for drills, furnaces, assemblers and drafting benches. Visible wiring, pole ranges, supply/demand readouts and fair shortages explain the network.
- Lost Orchard growing beds: restore up to three beds, supply real planks and fuel, collect produce and trade at the market. Manual delivery and inserters both work.
- Greybank road restoration: nine funded deliveries restore a visible regional road with a local travel benefit.
- Four named factory slots, validated manual/backup recovery and opt-in separate autosaves every five simulated minutes.
- Settlement map with saved exploration, named pins, tracking and reconstructed ore details after loading. Hover deposits to see remaining stock.
- Construction-plan supply checklists, queue-aware shortage crafting and bounded ghost undo/redo with Ctrl+Z/Ctrl+Y.
- Actionable production-stall navigation and a paid local workshop repair crew.
- Two protective coat upgrades in Engineer [P], plus regional keeper conversations and unread journal guidance.
- Ten bundled CC0 music recordings across five context playlists, with credits and license text included.
- Useful, wrapped hotbar tooltips explaining what each building does and why it helps.

## Existing worlds

Existing factories retain their progression and layouts. Electricity is an optional retrofit; original machines keep working. Older industrial worlds can now gather timber from finite trees and craft planks for regional projects. Loading a recovery copy does not modify files until you save.

## Validation and scope

The Windows packaging pipeline imports assets, runs the integrated checks, exports the game and smoke-tests the exported executable. Native UI/gameplay checks cover power upgrades, protection purchase, named slots, separate autosave recovery, map reconstruction and small-window hotbar tooltips.

This remains an early playable prototype. Deeper biome content, later town chapters, fluids, trains and multiplayer remain future work. The Windows executable is unsigned. The repository and release retain their existing visibility.
